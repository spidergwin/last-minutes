# Last Minutes: SaaS Marketing Video Strategy & AI Prompts

This document contains the professional video generation strategy for **Last Minutes**, a premium AI-driven transcription and meeting intelligence platform.

---

## 🚀 Product Positioning (Investor & User Grade)

Based on a deep analysis of the **Last Minutes** codebase, the following technical moats and brand identity are highlighted:
1. **Zero-Latency Architecture:** Direct-to-edge browser WebSockets.
2. **Nigerian Language Nuance:** Native support for Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and Pidgin.
3. **Meeting Bot Ecosystem:** Fully automated recording and diarization for virtual meetings.
4. **Hyper-Efficient Intelligence:** Powered by DeepSeek-V4-Flash.
5. **Brand Identity:** High-contrast **Amber/Orange** palette on a **Deep Charcoal** (#0a0a0a) background with minimalist grid/dot patterns.

## 🎨 Motion Graphics & Technical Specs

*   **Camera:** Use "Constant Velocity" pushes. No handheld movement.
*   **Transitions:** "Z-Axis Depth" transitions—camera moves *through* glass panes of the UI.
*   **Primary Color:** #f59e0b (Amber / HSL 25 95% 53%)
*   **Background:** #0a0a0a (Deep Charcoal / HSL 20 10% 4%)
*   **Typography:** Display: **Instrument Sans** | Body: **DM Sans**.
*   **UI Elements:** 64px Grid patterns, 24px Dot patterns, 10px Border Radius.
